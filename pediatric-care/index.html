Pediatric Care

Pediatric care with the whole child in mind

From the first weeks of life through the teenage years, Dr. Rainey's integrative approach looks beyond an isolated symptom to consider the child's health, development, and daily life. All services listed below are drafts and require his confirmation before launch.

  • Newborn Care
  • Well-Child Visits
  • Sick Visits
  • Immunizations
  • Growth & Development
  • Adolescent Care
01

Newborn Care

Early visits that support feeding, growth, and sleep — and give new parents room to ask everything on their list.

The first weeks with a newborn bring a great many questions. Newborn visits focus on checking your baby's growth and feeding, reviewing how the family is adjusting, and making sure parents leave with clear answers and a plan for the weeks ahead.

Confirm whether Dr. Rainey sees newborns, the timing of the first visit, and any hospital-related details.

02

Well-Child Visits

Regular checkups that follow growth and development from infancy through adolescence, with time for your questions.

Well-child visits are an opportunity to look at the whole picture: growth, development, sleep, nutrition, behavior, and school. They follow the recommended schedule of pediatric checkups and leave time for the questions parents bring with them.

Confirm visit schedule, length of visits, and what is included.

03

Sick Visits

Care for the fevers, coughs, rashes, and earaches of childhood, with clear guidance on what to watch for at home.

When your child is unwell, you want to be seen by someone who knows them. Sick visits address common childhood illnesses, with a careful examination and a clear explanation of what to expect and when to check back in.

Confirm same-day availability, after-hours guidance, and how families should reach Dr. Rainey when a child is sick.

04

Immunizations

Vaccines given on the recommended schedule, with honest, unhurried conversations about each one.

Immunizations are discussed openly, with time to talk through the recommended schedule and any questions or concerns you may have.

Confirm whether immunizations are offered in the office and Dr. Rainey's preferred wording for this section.

05

Growth & Development

Attention to milestones, learning, behavior, and emotional health as children grow into who they are.

Every child develops on their own timeline. Developmental check-ins look at speech, movement, learning, behavior, and emotional well-being, and help families decide together whether anything deserves a closer look or a referral.

Confirm the developmental and behavioral services Dr. Rainey offers and any screening tools used.

06

Adolescent Care

Respectful care for teens as they begin to take ownership of their own health, including school and sports physicals.

The teenage years bring new questions about health, growth, sports, and independence. Adolescent visits give teens a respectful space to ask their own questions, while keeping parents informed and involved.

Confirm age range served, sports and school physicals, and confidentiality approach for teen visits.

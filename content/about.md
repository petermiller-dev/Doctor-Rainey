## A physician, practicing independently

Dr. Shane Rainey is a Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine (DO) and a pediatrician caring for infants, children, and adolescents in Fort Worth. He practices independently from his office on Byers Avenue, where he has the freedom to give each family the time and attention a visit deserves.

Osteopathic physicians complete full medical training and are licensed to practice medicine in every specialty. Their education places particular weight on the whole person — on how health is shaped by growth, family, environment, and daily life. For a pediatrician, that perspective is a natural fit.

## How Dr. Rainey practices

Visits are not rushed. Dr. Rainey takes time to hear what parents have noticed, to examine carefully, and to explain clearly. When a question needs more than one conversation, he would rather follow up than leave a family guessing.

He sees parents as partners. You know your child better than anyone, and good pediatric care brings that knowledge together with medical training to make decisions that fit your family.

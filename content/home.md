## A pediatrician who takes the time to know your family

Dr. Shane Rainey is a Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine and a pediatrician caring for children in Fort Worth, Texas. He practices independently, which means the shape of each visit is guided by what your child needs rather than by a crowded schedule.

Osteopathic training emphasizes the whole person — how health, growth, home, and daily life fit together. That perspective informs how Dr. Rainey listens to parents, explains what he sees, and plans next steps alongside you.

Children are not small adults, and parents are not bystanders. Thoughtful pediatric care means listening carefully, examining carefully, and explaining clearly — so that you leave each visit understanding what was found, what it means, and what comes next.

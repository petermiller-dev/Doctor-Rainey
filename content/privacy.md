## What this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or contact forms. It does not ask for or store personal or medical information.

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives a standard request from your browser when a page loads; no cookies are set. The site is hosted by a third-party static web host, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and browser types) for security and reliability. Those logs are not used by this practice to identify visitors.

## Please do not send medical information online

Email and social media are not secure ways to share health information. Please do not send your child's medical details by email. Share health information during a visit, by phone, or through a secure method the office provides.

## Medical disclaimer

The information on this website is general and educational. It is not medical advice and does not replace an examination by a physician. Reading this website does not create a physician–patient relationship.

**If your child has a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.** For a possible poisoning, call Poison Help at [1-800-222-1222](tel:+18002221222). For a mental health crisis, call or text [988](tel:988).

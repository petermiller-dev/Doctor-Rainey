About Dr. Rainey

Shane Rainey, DO

A Fort Worth pediatrician who combines medical training with an integrative perspective and believes thoughtful care begins by listening to children and their parents.

A physician, practicing independently

Dr. Shane Rainey is a Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine (DO) and a pediatrician caring for infants, children, and adolescents in Fort Worth. He practices independently from his office on Byers Avenue, where he has the freedom to give each family the time and attention a visit deserves.

Osteopathic physicians complete full medical training and are licensed physicians. That education emphasizes caring for the whole person, including how health relates to growth, family, environment, and daily life. Dr. Rainey's interest in integrative and functional medicine adds another dimension to his approach to pediatric care.

How Dr. Rainey approaches care

Good pediatric care starts with understanding the child in front of you. Alongside symptoms and medical history, Dr. Rainey's integrative perspective considers nutrition, sleep, development, daily habits, and the questions families bring to the visit.

He sees parents as partners. You know your child better than anyone, and good decisions bring that knowledge together with careful medical evaluation, clear explanations, and discussion of appropriate options. Integrative care is meant to complement sound pediatric medicine, not replace necessary evaluation or treatment.

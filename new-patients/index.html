New Patients

Welcome. Here is how to begin.

Looking for a pediatrician who welcomes your questions and considers your child's health as a whole? This page explains the first steps; scheduling, membership, insurance, and pricing details await confirmation.

Getting started

  1. Reach out to the office. Call the office to introduce your family and ask about availability for new patients. Confirm the approved phone number and whether online scheduling will be offered.
  2. Schedule a first visit. A first visit is a chance for your child to be seen and for you to get to know Dr. Rainey and how he practices. Confirm whether a meet-and-greet or introductory visit is offered.
  3. Share your child's records. Bringing past records helps Dr. Rainey understand your child's history. Confirm how families should transfer records securely.
  4. Come in and settle in. Arrive a few minutes early for your first visit so there is time to get settled.
